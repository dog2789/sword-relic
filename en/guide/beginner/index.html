A Guide for New Adventurers

Controls, save statues and warps, potions and quick slots, and difficulty: what to know in your first 30 minutes

Learning the controls

Sword Relic is built so a few keys do everything. On PC, move with the arrow keys, slash with Z and jump with X. The double jump, pressing X again in midair, comes early in the adventure.

KeyWhat it does
←→Move. Walk to the edge of the screen to go to the next room.
↑↓Climb ladders and vines. ↑ in front of an NPC talks to them.
ZAttack. Also opens chests, breaks cracked walls and hits switches.
XJump. ↓+X drops through thin platforms.
CWeapon skill, different for every weapon (uses MP).
1~4Quick-slot potions.
I · MGear screen · world map.

If the keys don't suit you, change them under Settings below the game screen. In-game hints show your new keys too. On phones, put your thumb anywhere on the left side and a stick appears there; the three buttons on the right are attack, jump and skill. Turn your phone sideways for a much bigger screen.

Save statues and warps

Sword Relic saves automatically every time you enter a room. But when you close and reopen the game, you restart at the last save statue you touched. A save statue is a stone pillar with a glowing blue crystal floating above it. Touching one fully restores HP and MP and records that spot, so always stop by when you see one.

There are also warp pedestals around the world. Step close to light one up, and from any other warp pedestal you can press ↑ to teleport to any registered one. It saves a lot of time when you need to backtrack.

Tip

Carry a Return Feather from the General Store. Open the map (M) and press X to return to your last statue at any time. It's a lifesaver when you run out of potions deep in a dungeon.

Potions and quick slots

Quick slots 1 to 4 start with a Healing Potion, Mana Potion, Elixir and Power Tonic. Get potions from chests and monsters, or buy them at the General Store in your home village.

ItemEffectPrice
Healing PotionRestores 40 HP30 gold
Mana PotionRestores 20 MP40 gold
ElixirFully restores HP and MP150 gold
Power Tonic+30% attack for 30 seconds (lasts across rooms)120 gold
Return FeatherReturns you to the last save statue (not during boss fights)80 gold

When you fall

When your HP hits 0 you fall and restart at the last save statue. If you fell in a boss room, you can retry from that room's entrance. Your number of falls is recorded and shown at the ending, so be careful if you want bragging rights!

Choosing a difficulty

You can change the difficulty any time in Settings. There's no penalty either way, so pick whatever feels right.

  • Easy: half damage taken, monsters have 70% HP. For players who want to enjoy the story and exploration.
  • Normal: the balance the game was designed around.
  • Hard: 1.5× damage taken, monsters have 130% HP. In return you earn 25% more EXP and gold.

If screen shake makes you dizzy, set it to Low or Off in the same Settings window.
