Puzzle and Trap Tips

Crates and pressure plates, torches, timed switches, cracked walls, water, lava and wind: what to try when you are stuck

Last updated 2026-11-13

Most puzzles in Sword Relic are designed to be solved within a single room. If you're stuck, start by slowly looking around the whole room.

Wooden crates and pressure plates

Pressure plates on the floor don't go down when you stand on them. You have to push a nearby wooden crate onto them to open the iron gate. Push crates with your body, and if one gets stuck in a bad spot, hit it three times to send it back to where it started. Once an iron gate opens, it stays open.

Torches

Hit an unlit torch with your sword to light it. Some doors only open when every torch in the room is lit, so look for torches hidden up high or in corners.

Switches and timed switches

Hitting a switch makes colored blocks appear or disappear. Timed switches only open the way for a limited time, so decide where you'll run before you hit one. The screen tells you how many seconds you have.

Cracked walls

If a wall is cracked, hit it several times with your sword to break it. Some cracks are very faint, so getting into the habit of hitting walls when the way looks blocked really helps.

Natural hazards

  • Spikes: touching them hurts and sends you back to a safe spot.
  • Falling platforms: they collapse shortly after you step on them. Don't stop, keep crossing.
  • Water: when your head goes under, your breath gauge drops, and when it runs out you take damage.
  • Lava · quicksand: falling in hurts. In quicksand, jump quickly to escape.
  • Icicles: they shake for a moment when you get near, then fall. Once one starts shaking, don't stop underneath it.
  • Wind: it pushes you to one side. Wait for the moment it dies down.
Still stuck?

1. Check that you went to every edge of the room and that there's no exit above or below.
2. You may need new gear or an ability. Check the map (M) and explore directions you haven't been yet.
3. Try swapping accessories. Many places open up with a single ability.
