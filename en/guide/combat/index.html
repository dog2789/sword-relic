Combat Basics

Slashes and down-thrusts, weapon skills, invincibility frames, and the right mindset for boss fights

Last updated 2026-11-13

Keep slashes short and quick

Your basic attack is a short forward slash. You can keep moving while you swing, so hit and back off is the basic rhythm. Simply touching an enemy hurts you, so the safest distance is where only the tip of your blade reaches.

When you get hit, your body flickers for a moment: these are invincibility frames. You can't be hit again during that short window, so don't panic, just reposition.

Down-thrust

Press ↓+Z in midair to drive your sword downward. Stab an enemy like you're stepping on it and you bounce back up, so you can hit annoying ground monsters again and again from above. You can even use monsters as stepping stones to cross spikes.

Weapon skills (C)

Every weapon has its own skill on C. A sword wave that flies far, a triple flame, a slash that drains HP, an invincible dash: changing weapons changes how you fight. Skills use MP, so bring plenty of Mana Potions. You can read your current weapon's skill on the gear screen (I).

Tip

When you get a new weapon, don't just compare attack power, try its skill too. A weapon with slightly lower attack can be much easier to use if its skill fits the terrain or enemies.

Fighting bosses

Each boss repeats a few moves in a set rhythm. The first time you meet one, don't attack recklessly. First watch what tell it gives before each attack. Once you learn the order of tell, attack and opening, the same boss becomes surprisingly easy.

  • The moment a boss finishes an attack and catches its breath is your biggest chance.
  • Many bosses get fiercer as their HP drops. Don't relax at half health.
  • Some foes block attacks from the front. Try getting behind them.
  • You can't leave the room during a boss fight. Stop at a save statue and stock up on potions before going in.

If it's still too hard, gain a few levels, upgrade your weapon at the blacksmith, or switch to Easy for a while. That's fine too.
