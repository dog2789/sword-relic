A Tour of the World

From the meadows through the old-tree forest, caves, castle, ice fields, desert and lava fields. The mood and dangers of each region

Last updated 2026-11-13

The world of Sword Relic is one connected map. You walk from room to room with no loading screens between regions, and new abilities open places you couldn't reach before. The map (M) shows the rooms you've visited and how much you've explored.

Home village and meadows

The small village of Arden where the adventure begins, and the fields around it. The village has your mom, the blacksmith and the General Store. Gentle monsters like slimes and horned hares live in the fields, perfect for learning the controls. Still, there are hidden paths above the vines and past the hills, so look in every corner.

Old-tree forest

You cross a giant tree on branches and leaf platforms. Some stretches send you all the way back down if you fall, so it's great jump practice. Watch out for bees and mantises.

Caves

A dark underground of narrow passages. This is where spike traps, falling platforms and moving platforms first show up for real. You'll have to swim through flooded areas, and your breath gauge drops when your head goes under.

Old castle

A castle once guarded by knights long ago. High towers, long halls, torch puzzles and pressure plates, with ghosts that pass through walls and gargoyles frozen like stone waiting for you.

Ice fields

Slippery ice that makes it hard to stop, and icicles that fall from above. Gear that stops you from slipping makes it far easier.

Desert

A land of hot dunes and pyramids. Sink into quicksand and you'll slowly go under, taking damage. Vultures hunt from the sky and sand worms from below.

Lava fields

Volcanic ground boiling under your feet. Touching lava hurts a lot, and flame vents block the way. It's best to go in with fire-resistant gear.

Beyond

The story continues past the lava fields. See for yourself what lies there.
