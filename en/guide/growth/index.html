Growth and Gear

Levels, Life Crystals, blacksmith upgrades, set bonuses, and choosing accessories and consumables

Last updated 2026-11-13

Levels and Life Crystals

Defeating monsters earns EXP. Each level up gives +12 max HP, +5 max MP and +2 attack and fully restores HP and MP. Monsters get stronger in deeper regions, and they give more EXP too.

Life Crystals hidden around the map raise max HP by 20 each. They're often in high places, at what look like dead ends, or in rooms opened by solving puzzles.

Five gear slots

Gear has five slots: weapon, helmet, armor, boots and accessory. Gear isn't sold in shops; it only comes from treasure chests and bosses. The gear screen (I) works like a collection book, so you can see everything you've found at a glance.

Defense reduces damage by a ratio. With 40 defense you take about half damage. No amount of defense makes you invincible, so dodging is still the most important skill.

Set bonuses

Wear gear from the same set and you get a set bonus. For example, wearing the whole early Iron Knight Set (helm, armor, boots) adds ATK +3, DEF +2 and Max HP +20. Later sets have four pieces including a weapon, with much bigger bonuses. Set pieces are usually scattered across several rooms in one region, so search thoroughly.

Blacksmith upgrades

At the blacksmith in your home village you can spend gold to upgrade your weapon, helmet, armor and boots up to +8. Each weapon level adds +3 attack; armor gains defense.

Gold601202003204807009501300

Armor upgrades cost about 60% of the weapon upgrade at the same level. Upgrade levels are remembered per item, so invest in gear you'll use for a long time.

Tip

Once gold starts piling up, upgrades are the surest investment. But you may find a better weapon soon in a new region, so we recommend upgrading around the time you finish a region.

Choosing accessories

Some accessories just raise numbers, but many give special abilities: faster movement, clinging to walls to wall-jump, not being pushed by wind, and so on. Ability accessories can be the key to new paths, so if you're stuck, try swapping accessories.
