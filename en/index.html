Story

Long, long ago, there was a sword that protected the world. People called it the Sword Relic.

One day the sword lost its light and vanished, and soon monsters began to swarm the fields and forests.

In the small village of Arden, a boy unfolds an old map his grandfather left behind. At its edge, the mark of a sword. Now it is time to set out for the legendary sword.

Controls

PC

  • ←→ move, ↑↓ ladders
  • Z attack, X jump (double jump)
  • C weapon skill, 1~4 potions
  • I gear, M map

You can rebind keys in the in-game Settings.

Mobile

  • Move with the stick on the left
  • Attack, jump and skill with the buttons on the right
  • Hold it upright like a handheld, or turn it sideways for full screen

Add it to your home screen to play full screen like an app.
