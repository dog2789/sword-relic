Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-09

Sword Relic ("the site", swordrelic.com) treats your personal information with care and follows applicable privacy laws. This policy covers the site and the game (web, PC app and Android app).

1. Information we collect

The site has no sign-up and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email or phone number.

  • Game progress and settings: your save, character appearance, volume, key bindings, difficulty and so on are stored only in your browser's storage (localStorage) on your device and are never sent to us. The adventurer name you enter in the game is also stored only on your device.
  • Visit statistics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how often pages are visited. It uses no cookies and handles only aggregate data that can't identify you (page address, referrer, country, device type, etc.).
  • Access logs: our hosting provider (Cloudflare), font providers (Google Fonts, jsDelivr) and the app download host (GitHub) may process access logs such as IP addresses while providing their services.

2. Ads and cookies

The site may show Google AdSense ads. In that case cookies are used as follows.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • See How Google uses information from sites that use its services for details.

You can refuse cookies in your browser settings. The game works the same without them.

3. Retention and deletion

Progress and settings stored on your device remain until you delete them. You can delete them at any time with your browser's "clear site data" or by uninstalling the app. Before deleting, you can back them up on the Move Save page.

4. Children's privacy

Since the site does not directly collect personal information, it does not collect personal information from children either.

5. Contact

Send privacy questions to support@swordrelic.com.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the changes and the effective date on this page.
