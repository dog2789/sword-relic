PC (Windows)

Windows 10 · 11 (64-bit) · about 110MB

Get the installer Portable (no install)
  1. Double-click the downloaded file.
  2. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" window appears, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. The installer adds a desktop shortcut. The portable version just runs as is.

In game, ESC or the ⚙ at the top left opens Settings; F11 toggles full screen.

📱

Android

Android 7.0 or later · about 5MB

Get the APK
  1. Open this page on your phone and download the APK.
  2. Tap the downloaded file and allow installing from unknown sources.
  3. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway.

The warning appears because this is a pre-store release. Install new versions over the old one and your progress is kept.

iPhone · Mac · other devices

You can play right in your browser with nothing to install. On a phone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen and it opens full screen like an app.

Progress on the web, the PC app and the phone app is saved separately. To continue somewhere else, use Move Save.
