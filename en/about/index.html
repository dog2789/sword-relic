About Sword Relic

Sword Relic is a pixel metroidvania about leaving home in search of a legendary sword. The goal is to capture the feel of the pixel action games we loved as kids, playable right in your browser with nothing to install.

Every sprite, every sound effect and all the music were drawn and made in code. Every monster, every piece of gear and every room was made just for this game.

The maker

Sword Relic is designed and built by one person (Wooz). It keeps growing little by little thanks to everyone who plays and sends feedback.

Contact · feedback · bug reports

Send feedback, bug reports and any other questions below. When reporting a bug, include which room you were in, what you were doing and what happened, plus your device (PC or phone, and browser), so it can be fixed quickly.
